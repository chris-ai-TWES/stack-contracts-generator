🪄 Loan Agreement — AI Rewrite (full document)

The Loan Agreement is the one doc the AI can rewrite end-to-end — any section, not just add-ons. Describe the changes and it re-drafts the whole agreement (payment structure, balloon, security, defaults, whatever you say) using the real deal numbers, plus any non-standard terms pulled from a dropped LOI. The rest of the package stays untouched. e.g. "no payments for 2 years, then interest accrues to the balloon; make it a 3-year balloon; drop the new-entity clause; add a 5-day grace period and 5% late fee."
